Business

Charge once to connect, charge every year to run.

Who pays

  • Thermal Reserve (the physical clock): 3PLs, reefer fleets, cold stores. Entry product.
  • Commercial Reserve (the commercial clock): hypermarkets, hotels, caterers. Uses POS and ERP data to route and price stock. Comes second.
  • Buyer: head of supply chain. Must convince: food-safety / QA manager.

Why Qatar

  • Imports most of its food. Long sea and land legs, customs holds.
  • Summers past 45 °C: a fridge that fails at noon spoils a load in hours.
  • National Food Security Strategy 2030: food waste −50%, food loss −30%.
